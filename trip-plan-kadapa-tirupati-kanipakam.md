# 3-Day Trip Plan: Kadapa → Rayachoti → Thirumala → Kanipakam

**Duration:** 3 Days  
**Starting Point:** Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh  
**Route:** Kadapa → Rayachoti → Thirumala (Tirupati) → Kanipakam → Kadapa  
**Total Distance:** ~450 km (round trip)  
**Theme:** Hidden gems + Pilgrimage + Spiritual exploration

---

## Day 1: Kadapa → Rayachoti (~70 km | ~1.5 hrs drive)

### Morning (6:00 AM – 12:00 PM)
- **6:00 AM** – Depart from Kadapa
- **7:30 AM** – Stop at **Pushpagiri** (en route) – An ancient, less-visited temple town with a stunning hilltop Venkateswara temple. Offers panoramic views and fewer crowds than Tirupati.
- **9:30 AM** – Continue towards Rayachoti

### Afternoon (12:00 PM – 6:00 PM)
- **12:00 PM** – Arrive Rayachoti | Lunch at a local dhaba
- **2:00 PM** – Visit **Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Temple**, Rayachoti – a serene temple with beautiful sculpture work
- **3:30 PM** – Explore **Gandi Falls** (seasonal waterfall, ~15 km from Rayachoti) – an uncovered gem rarely on tourist maps
- **5:30 PM** – Visit **Yerramalai Forest Reserve** viewpoint for sunset

### Evening
- **7:00 PM** – Check in at Rayachoti (budget hotels or guest houses available near temple)
- Dinner + Rest

### Stay: Rayachoti

---

## Day 2: Rayachoti → Thirumala / Tirupati (~120 km | ~2.5 hrs drive)

### Morning (5:00 AM – 12:00 PM)
- **5:00 AM** – Early departure (to avoid temple crowds)
- **7:30 AM** – Arrive Tirupati base
- **8:00 AM** – Visit **Kapila Theertham** – a beautiful waterfall-temple combo at the foot of Tirumala hills; one of the most peaceful and underrated spots in Tirupati
- **9:00 AM** – Drive/Bus to **Thirumala** (23 km uphill)
- **10:00 AM** – **Sri Venkateswara Temple Darshan** (book SSD darshan tickets in advance at tirupati.aponline.gov.in)

### Afternoon (12:00 PM – 5:00 PM)
- **12:30 PM** – Lunch at TTD Annadanam or local restaurant in Thirumala
- **2:00 PM** – Visit **Silathoranam** – a natural rock arch near Thirumala, a geological wonder and less-visited spot
- **3:00 PM** – **Akasaganga Theertham** – a sacred waterfall on the Tirumala hills, often missed by tourists
- **4:30 PM** – Descend to Tirupati

### Evening
- **5:30 PM** – Visit **Sri Padmavathi Ammavari Temple**, Tiruchanur (~5 km from Tirupati) – the divine consort of Lord Venkateswara
- **7:00 PM** – Check in at Tirupati (many hotel options available)
- Dinner + Rest

### Stay: Tirupati

---

## Day 3: Tirupati → Kanipakam → Kadapa (~150 km | ~3 hrs drive)

### Morning (6:00 AM – 12:00 PM)
- **6:00 AM** – Depart from Tirupati towards Kanipakam
- **7:30 AM** – Stop at **Narayanavanam** (en route, ~30 km from Tirupati) – Visit the **Kalyana Venkateswara Swamy Temple**, a calm and serene pilgrimage spot that most visitors skip
- **9:00 AM** – Arrive **Kanipakam** (total ~70 km from Tirupati)
- **9:30 AM** – **Sri Varasidhi Vinayaka Swamy Temple, Kanipakam** – one of the most powerful Ganesha shrines in South India; the idol is believed to be self-growing

### Afternoon (12:00 PM – 4:00 PM)
- **12:00 PM** – Lunch near Kanipakam temple
- **1:00 PM** – Explore **Kanipakam reservoir** (adjacent to the temple area) – a tranquil, barely visited spot
- **2:30 PM** – Depart for Kadapa (~180 km | ~3.5 hrs)
- Optional stop: **Gandikota** (if time permits, ~60 km detour from the return route) – the "Grand Canyon of India," a spectacularly uncovered destination

### Evening
- **6:00 PM** – Arrive back in Kadapa

## Essential Tips

- **Darshan Tickets:** Book Tirupati SSD darshan tickets online at least 7–10 days in advance at tirupati.aponline.gov.in
- **Best Season:** October to February (avoid summer heat; monsoon makes Gandi Falls spectacular)
- **Dress Code:** Temples require traditional attire; men – dhoti/pant-shirt; women – saree/salwar
- **Transport:** Private car recommended for flexibility; shared cabs and APSRTC buses available
- **Accommodation:** Book Tirupati hotels early; TTD guest houses are affordable if available
- **Cash:** Carry cash for temple offerings, tolls, and local dhabas
